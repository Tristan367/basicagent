House rules

A private note telling the AI how you want it to talk to your child: what to encourage, what to leave alone, and what your family believes. It is added to the AI's instructions for everything your child does here, and it is the strongest say you have over the way it speaks to them.

You do not need to list the obvious. In child mode the AI is already told to keep everything safe, kind and age-appropriate, to refuse anything adult, violent, frightening or unkind and suggest they ask you instead, never to swear, and never to simply hand over homework answers. Your child cannot reach the internet through the browser it uses, or open your projects. Write here about the things that are particular to your family.

Some things people write
  • “We are a Christian family. Where a lesson naturally allows it, draw on scripture, and never tell her that God is not real.”
  • “He is nine and reads well below his age. Short sentences, one idea at a time, and check he is following before moving on.”
  • “Keep politics out of it entirely. If she asks, say it is something to talk to us about.”
  • “She gives up the second something breaks. Make a fuss of her when she keeps going, and never let a bug sound like a failure.”
  • “No war, no guns, no fighting in the games he makes, even cartoon ones.”

Your child will not see this. It is not shown on this page while child mode is on, and the AI is told not to read it out or say what is in it. That said, write it as though they might one day read it anyway — no instruction to an AI is a locked drawer.

It steers the AI; it does not command it. It will not be used to make the AI unkind or unsafe towards your child — those rules stay on underneath, whatever is written here.

Connect your AI

This app doesn't come with its own AI. Instead, it connects to an AI you already pay for, using an API key — a secret code that lets this app talk to that AI on your behalf. Adding an API key is a completely normal, standard thing to do: it's the official way to use these AIs directly, and it works out much cheaper than paying a middleman for a subscription.
